# Nore Valley Accountants: Frequently Asked Questions

Admin questions only. For anything about your own tax position, please book a consultation (see Services and Fee Guide).

**1. What are your office hours?**
Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:30, excluding public holidays.

**2. How quickly will you reply to my email?**
Within 2 working days. Around the October and November tax deadlines, replies may take up to 4 working days. Urgent Revenue matters are always handled first.

**3. How do I send you my documents?**
Upload them to the client portal, or email scanned copies or photos to the office. Please name each file clearly, for example `2025-bank-statements.pdf`. Don't post originals unless we ask for them.

**4. What documents do I need to send for my annual return?**
See the annual records checklist (section C for sole traders, section D for companies) in our Onboarding and Annual Records Checklists.

**5. When do you need my records?**
Sole traders: by 31 August for the previous tax year. Limited companies: within 3 months of your financial year end. Records received later carry a €100 priority-processing charge.

**6. When is my tax return due to Revenue?**
Revenue sets the filing and payment deadlines and publishes them on revenue.ie. We tell you your exact deadline and the amount payable before we file.

**7. I've received a letter from Revenue. What should I do?**
Forward it to us within 2 working days. Don't reply to Revenue yourself until we've reviewed it. If it mentions a penalty, an audit or a deadline, mark your email "URGENT – Revenue".

**8. How much will my return cost?**
Our Services and Fee Guide lists starting prices. We give a fixed-fee quote after a free 20-minute discovery call.

**9. How do I pay my invoice?**
By bank transfer or by card through the client portal. Invoices are due within 14 days.

**10. How do I book a meeting?**
Reply to any email from us or contact Sinéad Kehoe, our practice manager. Discovery calls are free. Advice consultations with a partner are €120 for 30 minutes.

**11. Can you answer a quick tax question by email?**
We don't give tax advice by email. We'll arrange a short consultation instead, so the answer fits your circumstances.

**12. Who is my accountant?**
Your engagement letter names your assigned accountant or partner. If you're unsure, ask Sinéad Kehoe.

**13. I've changed address or bank details. What do I do?**
Email us with the new details and, for address changes, a proof of address dated within the last 6 months.

**14. Why do you need my ID again?**
Anti-money-laundering law requires us to keep identification up to date. We may ask for updated documents from time to time.

**15. Will you ever ask for my ROS password?**
No. We never ask for passwords or one-time codes by email. We access your Revenue record through the tax agent link you approve.

**16. Can you give me a letter confirming my income for a mortgage or loan?**
Yes, for clients whose latest return we have filed. Allow 5 working days. There's a €60 fee per letter.
