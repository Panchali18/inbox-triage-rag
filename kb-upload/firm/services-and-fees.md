## How quotes work

1. A prospect books a free 20-minute discovery call.
2. Within 3 working days of the call, we send a written fixed-fee quote.
3. Work begins once the engagement letter is signed and onboarding documents are received.

A quote assumes the records are complete and arrive by our records deadline. Extra work is quoted before we do it.

## Payment terms

- Annual fees are invoiced when the return or accounts are filed. Payment is due within 14 days.
- Monthly services are invoiced on the first working day of each month.
- We accept bank transfer and card payments through our client portal.
- Records received after our records deadline (see engagement terms) carry a €100 priority-processing charge.
