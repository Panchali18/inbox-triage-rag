# How to register for tax as a new company

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Source | revenue.ie, "Registering for tax" |
| URL | https://www.revenue.ie/en/starting-a-business/registering-for-tax/how-to-register-for-tax-as-a-new-company.aspx |
| Saved on | 2026-10-15 |

To register for tax, you must have a Companies Registration Office (CRO) number issued by the CRO.

When you start a new company, you or your tax agent must inform Revenue.

If your company is represented by a tax agent, they must submit an online registration application on your behalf through the Revenue Online Service (ROS).

If your company is not represented by a tax agent, you must submit a:

- Form TR2 (for Irish resident companies)

or

- Form TR2 (FT) (for foreign companies).

The TR2 forms can be used to register for:

- Corporation Tax (CT)
- Employer Pay As You Earn (PAYE)
- Value-Added Tax (VAT)
- Relevant Contracts Tax (RCT).

For further information on how to send the forms, please see the ‘Additional Information’ section of Forms TR2 and TR2 (FT).

You will be given a Tax Reference Number (TRN) to use when trading and filing your tax returns.

## Filing returns and making payments

Once your company has been registered, you must file all payments and returns online through ROS.
